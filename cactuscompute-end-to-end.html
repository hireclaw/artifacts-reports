The thesis — from the Needle 2 post

"Edge AI has lately meant Macs and PCs, but the true edge is mostly cheap hardware… Most phones in emerging markets ship under $200. Count budget phones, Raspberry Pis, microcontrollers, wearables, small robots like Reachy Mini, and connected home devices — and roughly four in five edge devices cost under $200. That is the hardware Needle targets: no GPU, no NPU, a few dozen MB of RAM."

Their formulation: turning on a light doesn't need world knowledge or prose — it's retrieval-and-assembly (match tool, fill arguments, emit JSON). That's why 45M parameters suffice where chat needs billions.

One model, three capabilities
  • Tool calls — picks the right functions, fills every argument from the user's words; two asks, two calls in order; off-topic returns an empty list, not a guess.
  • Structured extraction — declare a shape, hand over messy text, get typed fields back; the decode grammar guarantees it parses. Generalises to classification.
  • Text embedding — same model returns vectors for local search, tool routing, near-duplicate merging.

03 · Intelligence ladders / the mechanism

One set of weights, every depth a model.

A watch, a Raspberry Pi and a flagship phone don't want the same model — they want the same behaviour at different sizes. Needle 3 is trained so any depth from 2 to 20 layers is a deployable model, picked at deploy time, not training time.

From the site · How the order is built — start with endpoints 0 & 19, repeatedly split the widest gap at its midpoint. Subnetwork d = first d entries. (blog/intelligence-ladders)
From the site · Training every depth at once — one path per optimizer step: full model with p=0.8, otherwise one depth drawn uniformly, loss scaled d/2L with a KL self-distillation pull toward the full model. (blog/intelligence-ladders)

Same bytes at every depth — a device holds the full file and picks its depth per request. Per-request depth switching: designed, not shipped yet (their words).

The numbers that matter
  • Ladder training is a regulariser — validation loss 1.958 vs 2.037 for the same architecture trained without a ladder; its 16-layer exit matches the control's full 18.
  • Fine-tune once, deploy any depth — the LoRA trains on frozen full-depth weights, merges, then slices: θd = slice(θ + BA, Sd).
  • Fine-tuning lifts every subnetwork +18–36 points on DroidCall; from 4 layers up the tuned model passes DeepSeek V4 Flash, a frontier cloud model.
  • The slice-of-slice trap — re-slicing a 16L slice by its own numbering yields 13 of 14 untrained subnetworks; fixed by carrying the parent's order in every slice.
  • 2L is honest — gated score ≈ 0 (withholds nearly everything); forced, it lands ~20.5 on DroidCall. "Two layers of a generalist is not a product" — but 2L fine-tuned on one product's tools reaches 56.5.
04 · The science / attention-only study

Delete the FFN. Measure what breaks.

The feed-forward network holds two-thirds of a transformer's parameters. Nobody had run the converse experiment — delete it, with parameters, compute and depth each controlled. Cactus did; Needle 2 and 3 are built on the answer (arXiv: A Controlled Study of Attention-Only Transformers).

From the site · What the FFN costs under three controls. The FFN's parameters matter; its functional form, on this distribution, largely does not. (blog/simple-attention-networks)
From the site · Where the gap lives — by 105B tokens the SAN leads on reasoning traces and answers, trailing only on query tokens: positions with nothing in context to route from. (blog/simple-attention-networks)
Why it trains at all

A SAN layer only selects and transports context — it can't synthesise what isn't there. The study found: QK-norm is load-bearing (removing it diverges — the one surprise), residual gates are performance-neutral but diagnostic, post-attention sandwich norm beats baseline by 0.009 nats, depth is U-shaped with a 20-layer optimum, and 48-layer attention-only stacks train without incident. Pipeline noise floor: 0.0015 nats; eight predictions registered in advance; the fineweb-edu gap was predicted at 0.02–0.05 and measured 0.040.

05 · The toolkit / four inventions

Every byte budgeted twice.

From the site · The .cact format — one little-endian file mapped into memory and read in place: 196-byte header (49 × u32 geometry), 112 B of Lloyd-Max codebooks, a nameless 44 B-per-tensor directory, blobs, tokenizer. No loader, no names, no parsing. (blog/cact-format)
From the site · Cactus Quants on one group of 128 weights — Walsh-Hadamard rotation makes every group look Gaussian, so one codebook fits all; store the norm (fp16) + indices. Cost: b + 1/8 bits per weight → 2.125 bits at CQ2. (blog/cact-format)
Hadamard MLP

Replaces dense FFN projections with three Kronecker-factored mixes that start as the Walsh-Hadamard transform and learn from there. 25.6K params/layer instead of 4.7M, about a fifth of the compute per token.

Engram memory

World knowledge moves out of the stack into hashed n-gram tables read a few rows per token. 8M params that cost no arithmetic — capacity nearly free at decode, which is battery.

Engine co-design

Weights never decompress into RAM — 2-bit codes expand inside vector registers, int8 end to end. Grammar pruning skips up to 98% of the vocabulary projection on structural tokens. One binary self-selects kernels (SDOT/NEON/AVX2/RISC-V/wasm).

Confidence gating

Every call ships with confidence = min(calibrated head, decode prob). Engine floor 0.1 withholds to suppressed_calls. Route in three bands: ≥0.7 act · 0.1–0.7 confirm · refuse. At 0.70: 19 acted, 1 wrong.

07 · Deployment / thirteen folders

One file, everywhere from a watch to x86.

Every target runs the same engine on the same weights. Thirteen platform folders, each an engine under 1 MB that loads needle3.cact at start. needle build --platform <folder> --layers <n> fetches it and writes the depth you choose.

From the site · The platform folders — one engine per folder, each loading the same needle3.cact. (blog/needle-supported-devices)
From the site · Picking a depth — decode on a Pi 5 runs ~4,000 tok/s at 2 layers down to ~400 at 20; prefill 10,000 → 1,000. (blog/needle-supported-devices)
Runtime surfaces
  • CLI — one query, or --serve HTTP on localhost; --forced bypasses the gate, --fail-input-overflow refuses instead of trimming.
  • C — needle.h: init / complete / embed; OpenAI-style schemas accepted; one process per fine-tune.
  • Browser — needle.js + needle.wasm; the live sandbox on their site runs it fully client-side.
  • WASI — signed component (cactus:needle/engine@3.0.0) on GHCR, cosign-verified, pullable with oras/wkg.
  • Air-gapped — copy the cache dir, HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1; nothing phones home.

08 · Platform & research / the pipeline

Customise on their infra. Read the papers.

The Cactus Platform (dashboard)

Needle was designed to be customised — a 2-layer subnetwork fine-tuned on one product's tools runs on devices far smaller than the full model needs. The platform is the full path: Cactus datasets, the 2-bit quantisation behind the shipped model, evaluation design and tracking, full-depth fine-tunes, dataset management — on their infrastructure and training pipeline. Or stay local: the Python package (cactus-needle) does inference, LoRA fine-tuning and export on your own Mac/PC in minutes to hours, then needle build ships a .cact for any platform. Tool schemas three ways: decorated functions, Pydantic models, raw JSON. triggers regexes pin intents that must always reach a tool.

09 · Verdict / independent read

What holds up, what to watch.

Genuinely strong
  • Real production deployment — Pebble's Index 01 runs Needle locally. That's rare proof, not a demo.
  • The science is falsifiable and mostly wins — registered predictions, seed-pair reproducibility, a 0.040 measured vs 0.02–0.05 predicted gap. The ladder result (1.958 vs 2.037) is a clean, checkable win.
  • Honest benchmark framing — they disclose both comparison asymmetries, publish where they lose (BFCL overall, DroidCall), and mark vendor-run evals as vendor-run.
  • End-to-end co-design is real — model, quantisation, file format, engine and kernels designed as one budget. 70 MFLOPs/token vs 164 same-shape.
  • Open model + source-available engine + local fine-tune — you can verify and own your deployment.
Watch-outs (their own caveats, plus ours)
  • Not a chat model — narrow agentic scope is the whole bet; MMLU-class tasks are at chance at these scales. Conversational needs stay in the cloud.
  • Confidence head is calibrated on the base model only — tuned weights report None; non-English is fragile (correct Spanish calls measured 0.0).
  • Per-request depth switching is designed, not shipped.
  • Benchmarks are small and partly vendor-run — DroidCall 200 rows, Mobile Actions 961; BFCL shows the out-of-distribution cliff (42.6 overall).
  • Platform lock-in risk is low but present — the dashboard path is their infra; the escape hatch (local Python) exists and matters.
One-line verdict

Cactus is the most credible "AI on $5 silicon" stack we've reviewed: the FFN-deletion science justifies the architecture, the ladder justifies the 8–29 MB claim, and Pebble justifies the production story. If your product speaks in tools rather than sentences, this is the default starting point — fine-tune the 4L rung first and measure on your own hardware.
